# Brand and story rules

## Brand identity

- The current public product name is **食卡卡**. Preserve these exact characters unless the user supplies an approved Latin or localized product name.
- Older working files may contain other project or product names. Treat those names as historical evidence only and never copy them into a new caption, filename, end card, metadata field, or public repository by default.
- Do not invent accuracy percentages, medical outcomes, weight-loss promises, nutritional diagnoses, or comparison claims. Show only what the current footage demonstrates and what the user has approved.
- Before delivery, check the video for visible notifications, account names, faces, locations, health data, or other private information.

## Message hierarchy

Prefer one promise per video. The established product story is:

1. Show a recognizable meal immediately.
2. Show the phone entering, framing, photographing, or scanning the meal.
3. Remove recognition waiting time unless the wait itself is the point.
4. Hold the calorie and macro result long enough to read.
5. When the campaign is about retention rather than discovery, show adding the meal to the food log and opening daily nutrients.
6. Close with 食卡卡 or an approved CTA.

## Proven story shapes

Use these as starting ranges and adjust to the footage:

| Mode | Recommended shape | Typical runtime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rapid scan | Meal 1.5–2.2s → scan 1.8–3.8s → result 3–6s → brand close | 9–13s |
| Feature funnel | Meal → scan → result → add meal → nutrients → brand close | 15–20s |
| Comparison episode | For each food: reveal → action → result, repeated with a consistent rhythm | 20–45s |

Open on the clearest food frame, not an empty plate or hand setup. Prefer the later, steadier result take when it improves legibility. Do not reuse old source timestamps; find equivalent beats in the current source.

## Caption style

- Default campaign language: follow the brief. If it is genuinely unspecified, use the language already established by the supplied campaign assets and flag the choice in the strategy.
- Use short functional copy, normally one or two lines. Keep one idea per card.
- For English Latin copy, prefer a licensed Poppins Bold file when supplied or already present in the project.
- For Chinese copy or the 食卡卡 wordmark, use the approved brand font or a verified licensed CJK font. Confirm that all Chinese glyphs render; do not silently replace missing glyphs.
- Default treatment: centered in the upper safe area, white text with a strong black outline, high contrast, and consistent line spacing.
- Useful English patterns include `JUST SNAP YOUR MEAL`, `CALORIES & MACROS IN SECONDS`, `ADD IT TO YOUR FOOD LOG`, and `CHECK YOUR DAILY NUTRIENTS`. Adapt them to the current proof; do not force every line into every video.
- The last card should use the current approved brand name, not a historical placeholder.

## Picture and sound

- Default to clean hard cuts and product-first framing. Avoid decorative effects that compete with the food or result screen.
- Preserve aspect ratio. Scale and pad only when cropping would hide the phone, food, or result; use a deliberate crop when it improves clarity.
- Visual-only demos may mute the source track. Retain purposeful taps or movement sounds only if they are clean and approved.
- Add BGM only from a user-supplied or licensed source. Trim it to the exact timeline and use short fades at the beginning and end.
- Do not add a dynamic information card by default. Use one only when the brief needs a fact that the footage cannot communicate clearly.
